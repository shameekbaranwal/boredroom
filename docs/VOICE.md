# Voice & nomenclature

Boredroom plays your friend group as an over-serious company board that meets every
day to discuss Wordle. The joke is deadpan: office-memo language applied to emoji
grids, never winking at it.

## Rules

1. **Nouns and moments get the voice. Buttons stay plain.** A screen can be called
   "Agenda", but the button says "Join", "Approve", "Add a game". Nobody should have to
   decode a control.
2. **Dry, never mean.** "On a PIP" is a joke between friends; it is never aimed at
   someone outside the room or used to shame.
3. **A few lines per screen.** Headings, status chips, notifications and empty states.
   Not every label.
4. **Everything is lowercase.** All in-app text, including names, board names,
   headings, stamps and notifications ("soup daily", "ivan", "filed"). The
   lowercase keeps the corporate words from sounding like an actual corporation.
5. **Members are members.** "Board members" in copy, "members" anywhere plain is
   clearer. Never "directors" or "employees".

## Ideas held back

Considered and kept out until we see how the core voice lands:

- Guests as "contractors" who go "full-time" when they sign in. Funny, but makes
  guests sound second-class.
- Adding a game as "tabling a motion". Too much.
